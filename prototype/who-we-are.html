Who We Are

Founded in 2006, Felidae Conservation Fund protects wild cats and the vital ecosystems they sustain. Driven by a dedicated team of wildlife biologists, ecologists, and quantitative modeling experts, we combine rigorous field research with community engagement to deliver measurable conservation results.

We operate with high fiscal efficiency, channeling resources directly into on-the-ground research, habitat preservation, and public education. From analyzing wildlife movement patterns with predictive modeling to empowering residents with tools for peaceful coexistence, our team turns cutting-edge science into actionable, lasting environmental protection.

Meet Zara

Zara McDonald, MS MBA

President & Biologist, Director

Favorite Felid: Mountain Lion

Zara is a biologist, serial entrepreneur and conservationist. She is the founder and CEO of Felidae, a global conservation organization that conducts innovative research on wild felid species.

Zara brings over 20 years of experience in the fields of biology, conservation, and entrepreneurship. She has conducted groundbreaking research on felids throughout the world, and as a National Geographic Explorer, she is committed to raising awareness around biodiversity, wild felids and the link to public health. Her dedication to excellence is evident in her organization’s consistent record of publishing rigorous applied scientific research, advancing our understanding of the natural world.

Zara is a highly sought-after speaker and thought leader and has featured in numerous publications, including The New York Times, The Wall Street Journal, and National Geographic.
