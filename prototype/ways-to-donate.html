Donate With Your Everyday Activities

Felidae now has a listing on RoundUp.org, a platform partnered with Visa and Mastercard that lets supporters automatically donate the spare change from every swipe of their card.

All you need to do is assign Felidae Conservation Fund to your Visa or Mastercard on the RoundUp.org website (they are an Approved Financial Partner of Visa andMastercard):

Follow this link

That’s it! Signup takes less than 1 minute.

Millions of people are rounding up at the grocery store every day to support charities they don’t even know very well.

Now you can direct your round ups to a charity you love: Felidae Conservation Fund. Every time you spend, you’ll be giving a small amount to support us here at Felidae.

What could feel better than that?

Thank you for your continued support!
