Need an idea?

Felidae participates in and presents at the following types of events:

  • Annual Community Meetings
  • Board Meetings
  • Charity Rides or Runs
  • Corporate Lectures
  • Docent and Volunteer Trainings
  • Environmental Forums
  • Exhibit Events
  • Library Lectures
  • Nature Walks
  • Neighborhood Presentations
  • Private Events
  • Science Fairs
  • Scientific Conferences
  • Zoological Society Lecture Series

If you don’t find your event on this list, suggest something to us. We receive many eccentric requests.
